Maharashtra | Engagement Journey
Six engagements, one forest-economy pathway

From community capability to public platforms and model demonstration — a connected pathway for Maharashtra’s forest economy.

01
Foundation
Community capability + evidence
  • Korchi: trained Gram Sabha members in CFR boundary mapping and forest-inventory collection; supplied CFR maps and economic-species distribution layers.
  • Studies in Gondia and Gadchiroli examined CFR co-benefits and GST impacts on the kendu economy.
02
Public Platforms
State and national engagement
  • Co-organised national bamboo conventions in Delhi and Mumbai with Shri Pasha Patel; joined MITRA’s consultation for the ADB bamboo-economy rollout.
  • An MoU for forest-resource inventory and renewed engagement with Gadchiroli ZP, Forest Department and district administration on bamboo value-chain action.
03
Demonstration
Model demonstration + policy dialogue
  • Through an MoU with Nandurbar district administration, demonstrated a women-led sustainable bamboo supply chain in CFR areas and began strengthening CFRMC capacity.
  • Presented the industry potential and community-centric model to Hon’ble Minister Shri Ashish Jaiswal, Co-Guardian Minister of Gadchiroli.
